- [ ] 10.3.4 Exporting the whole catalogue (our own "dump", like MusicBrainz/Discogs) — is that planned? Directly tied to the data licence ([13.1](13-legal.md)).
- [ ] 10.3.5 Export privacy: what is excluded from a public collection export (prices, notes, purchase places), what is available only to the owner.
